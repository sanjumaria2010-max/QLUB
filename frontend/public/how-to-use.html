05 / 10 · Prospect Logic
How we classify a Qlub Prospect

The 3-rule qualification

Click "How we classify a Qlub Prospect" in the source panel to expand the explainer. A venue must meet all three:

  • Sit-down / full-service — Qlub is a pay-at-table product.
  • Modern POS with an integration path (Toast, Micros, Square, Clover, Aloha, TouchBistro) — or unverified but format fits, so an SDR can confirm in a 2-minute call.
  • ≥ 1 close-out / billing / wait-time pain point in public reviews — the exact friction Qlub removes.

Cash-only / food-truck / pop-up venues are marked Anti-Segment because there's no POS to integrate with.

06 / 10 · Bring Your Own Data
Restaurant Expo · Import Excel

Drop in your own list

Have an Excel sheet from a prior research sprint? Click IMPORT EXCEL (purple button, downward arrow) and the table re-populates instantly with your rows. Filters, table sort, and exports all work on the imported data.

  • Supported: .xlsx, .xls, .csv
  • Recognized columns (case-insensitive): Name, Neighborhood, Region, Cuisine, Valuation, POS, Segment
  • Click CLEAR IMPORT (X icon) to revert to the live LA dataset.
  • Then export back out as PDF or XLSX.

08 / 10 · Voice of Customer
Page 4 — What LA diners actually say

211 real, cited quotes

Static snapshot of real customer reviews from Google, Yelp, Eater, Reddit, TripAdvisor — pulled via Gemini googleSearch grounding. Every quote is citeable.

  • 3 nested filters: Location → Restaurant → Issue.
  • 5 KPI tiles: Total quotes, POS Friction, Wait Time, Billing, Tipping.
  • Each card shows restaurant, source, date, severity stars, the quote, and a VIEW link to the source.
  • Hover any truncated text for the full string.
  • Export the current view as CSV, PDF, or XLSX.
09 / 10 · Outreach Agent
Voice of Customer · Pitch Generator

From insight to email — instantly

The Closing Console sidebar is locked until you narrow to one specific restaurant. That guarantees the email is grounded in real, cited pain points for that venue.

  • Pick a Location (e.g. Eastside).
  • Pick a Restaurant in that region (e.g. All Day Baby).
  • Sidebar flips to READY and shows the venue name.
  • Click GENERATE OUTREACH — Gemini drafts a subject + 120-word body.
  • Click COPY EMAIL and paste into Gmail / Outreach / Apollo.
10 / 10 · Marketing Insights
Page 5 — Pain → Revenue Recovery

Quantify the opportunity

Translate the pain points into a dollar number. Use the Potential Recovery Calculator to model annual revenue recovered by removing checkout friction at any venue.

  • Slide the Total Tables, Avg. Check, and Check-drop Delay sliders.
  • Annual Recovery Goal updates live (e.g. $112,200 at 25 tables / $85 / 12-min delay).
  • Pain Point Spectrum ranks the top 4 cited issues by intensity.
  • Neighborhood Specifics shows where the pain (and therefore the upsell) is highest.
